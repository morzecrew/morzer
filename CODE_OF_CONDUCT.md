# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique work, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people newer to the problem.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Focus on what is best for the project.
- Show empathy toward other people.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Anything else a reasonable person would recognise as unprofessional.

## Scope

Applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately, through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/morzecrew/morzer/security/advisories/new)
if you would rather it not be public. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers who do not follow this in good faith may face consequences decided
by the project's other maintainers.
